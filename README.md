# iPhoneXR Troubleshooting Summary

---

The iPhone had two main issues: its battery drained quickly, and its charging port could not detect the Lightning cable when connected.

First, I tested the charging port with a known-good cable to confirm the problem was not caused by the cable. The phone still had trouble charging, which pointed to an issue with the charging port.

Next, I checked the iPhone's Battery Health. In system settings, the battery capacity was 75%, indicating significant degradation and reduced holding capacity.

Dismantling the phone to replace the parts was daunting, as some components are very sensitive.

After replacing the parts, the phone's battery capacity read 100% in the system settings, and the phone detected the Lightning flex cable when connected.

---

## What I Learned

---

This repair demonstrated the importance of testing before replacing parts. Using a known-good cable and replacement components helped identify the actual faulty parts instead of assuming the problem was caused by the phone itself.
